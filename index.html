About the Journal

International Journal of Psychological Community

Humanities and Social Sciences · Research, Knowledge & Community Well-Being

The Tamil Nadu Psychology Association – Journal of Psychological Community is an international-level, peer-reviewed, open-access academic interdisciplinary journal dedicated to promoting research and knowledge in psychology and related social sciences. The journal is published quarterly.

Published by the Tamil Nadu Psychology Association (TNPA) in research collaboration with the Indian Institute of Psychological Research and Training (IIPRT), the platform welcomes original research papers, review articles, case studies, and book reviews from faculty members, researchers, practitioners, and students worldwide.

Transparent Editorial Workflow

Author Submission Process

A clear four-stage publication pathway guided by APA 7th Edition standards and impartial double-blind peer review.

Author Guidelines & Fees
01

Prepare in APA 7th Style

Format your original manuscript using our step-by-step APA 7th Edition modules, with similarity index verified below 10–20%.

02

Register & Submit Online

Complete the manuscript submission form with your Word document, abstract summary, and author affiliation details.

03

Double-Blind Peer Review

Submissions undergo editorial screening followed by impartial subject-expert peer review evaluating rigor and ethics.

04

Acceptance & Publication

Upon acceptance, submit the signed Combined Submission Form, plagiarism report, and fee receipt for open-access publication.
